✅ Affiliate Marketing Do’s (Compliant with UAE Law)

1. Obtain Proper Licensing (if promoting from within the UAE)

Affiliates operating in the UAE must hold an e-media license from the National Media Council (NMC).
Freelancers or influencers also require a valid UAE trade license if monetizing their promotions.
2. Disclose Paid Promotions Clearly

Always mark paid promotions with clear disclosures such as #Ad, #PaidPartnership, or #Sponsored in both English and Arabic (e.g., #إعلان).
3. Use Only Approved Marketing Materials

Use only the official branding, images, videos, and promotional text provided or approved by Desert Jewel Realty.
4. Collect and Handle Leads Legally

Always obtain the lead’s consent before submitting their personal information.
Respect privacy under UAE PDPL (Personal Data Protection Law) — leads have a right to data access and deletion.
5. Comply with Cultural Sensitivities

All marketing content must respect UAE culture, religion, values, and decency standards. Avoid nudity, alcohol, religious offenses, or political content.
6. Use Honest and Accurate Claims

Represent Desert Jewel Realty services truthfully. Do not exaggerate property prices, investment returns, or legal status.
7. Follow Anti-Spam and Telemarketing Laws

Only contact people who’ve opted in. Avoid cold calls, bulk messages, or unsolicited emails unless permitted and compliant with UAE law.
8. Monitor Your Campaigns

Be proactive in updating or removing outdated, false, or misleading campaigns.
❌ Affiliate Marketing Don’ts (To Avoid Legal Issues)

1. Don’t Operate Without a License (if in UAE)

It is illegal to earn income from promotional activity inside the UAE without a proper license.
2. Don’t Hide Paid Promotion

Failure to disclose payments (even gifts or commissions) in your promotions can lead to heavy fines and suspension.
3. Don’t Use Deceptive Practices

No clickbait, false urgency (“only 1 left”), misleading testimonials, or fake scarcity tactics.
4. Don’t Collect or Sell User Data

You are strictly prohibited from selling, sharing, or misusing personal data of leads.
Do not submit fake or self-generated leads—this will lead to immediate termination and legal action.

6. Don’t Impersonate the Company

Never present yourself as an employee or agent of Desert Jewel Realty. You are an independent marketing partner only.
7. Don’t Violate Social Media Platform Rules

If you’re promoting on Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube, follow their rules for affiliate disclosures and ad policies.
8. Don’t Engage in Black Hat Techniques

No fake reviews, fake traffic, automated bots, cloaking, or cookie stuffing. These are grounds for ban and legal escalation.
